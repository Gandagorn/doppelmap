    About Doppelmap

    Doppelmap maps face-similarity between public figures. Every photograph comes from Wikimedia Commons and is turned into a 512-number ArcFace face embedding (InsightFace buffalo_l); each person is reduced to a single consensus face, and UMAP lays those out so people who look alike sit near each other.

    Pick anyone to see their closest matches. The ⇄ button shows the two photographs that actually match — not two arbitrary portraits, but the specific pair the model rates highest.

    Similarity is raw cosine distance between faces. It runs lower than intuition suggests: photos of the same person typically score above 0.35, so two different people at 0.30 is already a striking resemblance.

    Images are CC-licensed or public domain, credited on each photo. People are drawn from the most-viewed English Wikipedia biographies; fictional characters are excluded.
